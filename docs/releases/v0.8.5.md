# v0.8.5

- Automatically move sessions whose brokers have exited unexpectedly into History, with their retained output.
- Fix `tdock stop` for sessions whose brokers have already exited. A broker that still owns its lock keeps its session during a connection failure.
- Rename the Stop tab to History in all five languages.

Automatic recovery also runs when history expiry is disabled. Exit and reopen any open TaskDock interface to load the new tab label.
